      이 루틴의 근거Evidence for this routine
      1. Stemple JC et al. (1994). Efficacy of vocal function exercises as a method of improving voice production. J Voice 8(3):271–278. VFE 4단계 루틴의 원전.The original source of the four-step VFE routine.
      2. Angadi V, Croake D, Stemple J (2019). Effects of Vocal Function Exercises: A Systematic Review. J Voice 33(1). 21편 분석. 정상 목소리, 직업적 음성 사용자 포함 모든 연구에서 긍정적 효과, 부작용 보고 없음.21 studies. Positive effects in every study, including normal voices and professional voice users; no adverse effects reported.
      3. Titze IR (2006). Voice training and therapy with a semi-occluded vocal tract: rationale and scientific underpinnings. J Speech Lang Hear Res 49(2):448–459. 빨대, 립트릴, 허밍이 왜 성대 부담을 줄이는지에 대한 이론.The theory of why straws, lip trills and humming reduce the load on the vocal folds.
      4. Pozzali I et al. (2024). Effectiveness of SOVTEs in Patients with Dysphonia: Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. J Voice 38(1). 환자 대상 근거의 질은 매우 낮음. 워밍업 용도로만 짧게 씀.Evidence quality in patients is very low. Used here only as a short warm-up.
      5. Roy N et al. (2003). Three treatments for teachers with voice disorders: a randomized clinical trial. J Speech Lang Hear Res 46(3):670–688. 공명 치료는 효과, 호흡근 훈련은 유의한 개선 없음.Resonance therapy helped; respiratory muscle training gave no significant improvement.
      6. Yiu EM et al. (2017). A systematic review of resonant voice therapy. Int J Speech Lang Pathol 19(1):17–29. 근거 수준 중간, 대규모 연구 부족.Moderate level of evidence; large studies are lacking.
      7. Elliot N, Sundberg J, Gramming P (1995). What happens during vocal warm-up? J Voice 9(1):37–44. 워밍업 효과는 사람마다 차이가 큼.Warm-up effects vary a lot between people.
      8. Gish A et al. (2012). Vocal warm-up practices and perceptions in vocalists. J Voice 26(1). 가장 흔한 워밍업은 5–10분, 5음 스케일과 글리산도.The most common warm-up is 5–10 minutes of five-note scales and glissandos.
      9. Picheny MA, Durlach NI, Braida LD (1985, 1986, 1989). Speaking clearly for the hard of hearing I–III. J Speech Hear Res. 또렷한 말은 단어 사이 쉼, 늘어난 소리 길이, 덜 줄어든 모음, 끝 자음 발음이 특징. 속도만으로는 설명되지 않음.Clear speech has pauses between words, longer sounds, less reduced vowels and released final consonants. Speaking rate alone doesn't explain it.
      10. Munro MJ, Derwing TM (1995). Foreign accent, comprehensibility, and intelligibility in the speech of second language learners. Language Learning 45(1):73–97. 억양이 강해도 충분히 알아들을 수 있음. 억양과 이해도는 부분적으로 별개.A strong accent can still be fully understandable. Accent and comprehensibility are partly independent.
      11. Derwing TM, Munro MJ, Wiebe G (1998). Evidence in favor of a broad framework for pronunciation instruction. Language Learning 48(3):393–410. 운율(강세, 리듬, 억양) 중심 훈련이 즉흥 발화의 이해도를 높임.Prosody-focused training (stress, rhythm, intonation) improved the comprehensibility of spontaneous speech.
      12. Field J (2005). Intelligibility and the listener: The role of lexical stress. TESOL Quarterly 39(3). 단어 강세가 틀리면 원어민과 비원어민 청자 모두 알아듣기 어려워짐.Misplaced word stress makes speech harder to understand for both native and non-native listeners.
      13. Foote JA, McDonough K (2017). Using shadowing with mobile technology to improve L2 pronunciation. Journal of Second Language Pronunciation 3(1):34–56. 16명이 8주간 주 4회 이상, 10분씩 쉐도잉하며 자기 녹음. 즉흥 발화의 이해도와 유창성은 좋아졌고 억양(accent)은 그대로. 소규모 연구.16 learners shadowed for 10 minutes at least 4 times a week for 8 weeks, recording themselves. Comprehensibility and fluency of spontaneous speech improved; accent did not change. Small study.
      14. Hardison DM (2004). Generalization of computer-assisted prosody training: Quantitative and qualitative findings. Language Learning & Technology 8(1):34–52. 음높이 곡선을 보여 주는 운율 훈련이 새 문장에도 일반화됨.Prosody training with visual pitch curves generalized to new sentences.
      15. Hamada Y (2016). Teaching EFL Learners Shadowing for Listening. Routledge. 쉐도잉의 듣기 효과에 대한 연구 정리.An overview of research on shadowing for listening.
      16. Maas E et al. (2008). Principles of motor learning in treatment of motor speech disorders. Am J Speech Lang Pathol 17(3):277–298; Austermann Hula SN et al. (2008). J Speech Lang Hear Res 51(5). 매번 즉시 피드백보다 줄이거나 늦춘 피드백이 기억에 더 오래 남음 (말 영역의 근거는 아직 제한적).Reduced or delayed feedback is retained longer than immediate feedback after every attempt (evidence for speech is still limited).
      17. Alves M et al. (2019). The Effect of Hydration on Voice Quality in Adults: A Systematic Review. J Voice 33(1). 물 섭취는 도움, 카페인은 뚜렷한 악영향 없음.Drinking water helps; caffeine shows no clear harm.
      18. Hunter EJ, Titze IR (2009). Quantifying vocal fatigue recovery. Ann Otol Rhinol Laryngol 118(6):449–460. 많이 쓴 뒤 회복에는 시간이 걸림. 짧게 자주 연습.Recovery after heavy use takes time. Practise in short, frequent sessions.
